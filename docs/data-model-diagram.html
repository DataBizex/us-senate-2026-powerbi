Why no relationship on the projected winner. FactRaceResult holds projected_winner_fec_cand_id and leading_candidate_fec_cand_id, but neither is used as a relationship: a second path from DimCandidate to FactRaceResult would make the model ambiguous. Winner lookups go through the flags DimCandidate[is_leading_candidate] and FactCandidateResult[is_leading_candidate] instead.

Before you build anything on it: 33 of 35 races have a published count and six come from the state's own election authority, but only 3 have margin_safe_to_use = Y. Massachusetts, Illinois and Rhode Island still rest on partial counts. No result is certified. All 210 ratings are quarantined and must stay out of forecast measures.
