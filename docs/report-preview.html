Money against outcome exploratory

Exploratory only, not statistical evidence. n = 33 races with any count, of which 6 carry an official count and 3 a complete one. Point opacity encodes count quality. No causal claim is made or supported: campaign finance here stops on 14 October, the election was 3 November.

Faint points are races whose count is partial or unverified. They are plotted so the picture is not cherry-picked, but they carry no weight.

What is deliberately left out

  • Leadership PACs — appear on FEC candidate pages but are not authorized committees. Not candidate money.
  • Party committees and independent expenditures — spent on a candidate's behalf, not received by them.
  • Internal transfers — money moved between a candidate's own committees. Present for 79 filers, $200.4M in total. Counted once here, never twice.
  • Ohio caveat — the FEC reports Sherrod Brown's activity under a PAC that is not an authorized committee, so his candidate total may understate.
286 of 619 filers state no reporting period at all. A blank there means no activity was reported, not zero raised.
